What is the Constitution?

The foundation of the world's largest democracy.

📖

Our Constitution

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the country. It defines the structure of government, protects fundamental rights and establishes the principles of democracy, justice, liberty and equality.

At Bharathiy Savidhan, our goal is to make constitutional knowledge easier and more accessible for everyone.

CONSTITUTIONAL KNOWLEDGE

Important Articles

Explore some important provisions of the Constitution.

ARTICLE 14

Equality Before Law

Equality before the law and equal protection of the laws within the territory of India.

ARTICLE 15

Prohibition of Discrimination

Prohibits discrimination on specified grounds such as religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth.

ARTICLE 16

Equality of Opportunity

Provides equality of opportunity in matters relating to public employment.

ARTICLE 17

Abolition of Untouchability

Untouchability is abolished and its practice in any form is prohibited.

ARTICLE 19

Freedom of Speech

Protects important freedoms of citizens, subject to constitutional restrictions.

ARTICLE 21

Right to Life & Liberty

Protects life and personal liberty according to the procedure established by law.

A JOURNEY THROUGH HISTORY

Constitution Timeline

1946

The Constituent Assembly began its historic constitutional journey.

1947

India became independent and the Assembly continued the task of constitution-making.

26 November 1949

The Constitution of India was adopted by the Constituent Assembly.

26 January 1950

The Constitution came into force and India became a Republic.
